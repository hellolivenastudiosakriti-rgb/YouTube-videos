## 3. AGE / TIMELINE GUIDE (for consistent aging)
* Ganga leaves → Shantanu's **36 years** alone (1.94.18) → Devavrata found (~36) → **4 years** yuvarāja (1.94.40) → vow (~40).
* Vicitravīrya: married **7 years** before death (1.96). Niyoga → Dhṛtarāṣṭra/Pāṇḍu/Vidura.
* Pāṇḍavas brought to Hastinapura **17 days** after Pāṇḍu's death (1.117.27).
* Lākṣāgṛha: reach Vāraṇāvata on **8th day of Phālguna, Rohiṇī** (1.133.30); live there **one year** (1.136.1).
* Exile: **12 years forest + 1 year incognito**; Bhīṣma's count: **13 years + 5 months + 12 nights** elapsed (4.47).
* War: **18 days**; Bhīṣma lies **58 nights** on arrows; dies in **Māgha, bright fortnight** (13.153.27–28).
* Dhṛtarāṣṭra lives **15 years** after the war (15.4.12). Yādava destruction in the **36th year** (16.1; Gāndhārī's curse 11.25).
* Ashwatthāman's curse: **3,000 years** (10.16.10). Parīkṣit rules **60 years** (10.16.14).

## 4. WORLD BIBLE (production design guardrails)
* **Era look:** Painted Grey Ware Iron Age (c. 1100–800 BCE) as *design reference* — mud & mud-brick, sal timber, lime plaster, hand-loom cotton, wild silk, deerskin (ajina), clay oil-lamps. No marble palaces, no stitched sherwanis, no plastic gold.
* **Chariots:** light timber, **spoked wheels**, horses (never Sinauli solid disks). Banner poles tall, emblems listed above.
* **Conch:** **real seashell** (Turbinella pyrum) — never "bronze".
* **Dice:** CE gives no material for the Sabhā; Kaṅka's dice are **golden, beryl-like** (4.6.1). Vibhītaka nuts = Vedic-tradition design choice.
* **Divine events, grounded:** flower-rain (wind-blown real petals), sky-voice (spatial whisper, no glow), garment miracle (only fabric physics), Viśvarūpa (5.129: smoke-flame from eyes/ears/nose, sun-rays from pores — practical fire/light, no neon).
* **Swayamvara target:** golden target + aerial machine with an aperture, five arrows — **no fish**.
* **Khāṇḍava:** two chariots circling opposite sides of the forest (1.217.1).
* **Arjuna's chariot at Kurukṣetra:** white horses; Kṛṣṇa holds the reins; ape banner — and at the very end it burns to ash (9.61).
