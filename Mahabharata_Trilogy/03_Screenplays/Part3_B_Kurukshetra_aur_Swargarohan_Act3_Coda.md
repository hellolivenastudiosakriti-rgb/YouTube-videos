# ACT III — "सूर्यास्त" (1:55:00 – 2:40:00)

### SEQ 9 — कर्ण सेनापति (1:55 – 2:03) `[CE 8.1–8.49]`
**SCENE 9.1 — KAURAVA CAMP — NIGHT 15**: **Karṇa** anointed commander `[CE 8.6]`. *"कल मैं अर्जुन को मारूँगा — पर मेरा सारथी कृष्ण के बराबर हो।"* — **Śalya**. The proud king of Madra is insulted; Duryodhana kneels to him and tells how **Brahmā himself drove Śiva's chariot** against the Three Cities `[CE 8.24]`. Śalya agrees — on one condition: *"जो चाहूँ, बोलूँगा।"*
**SCENE 9.2 — THE RIDE OUT — DAY 17**: Śalya **mocks Karṇa all the way** — the crow who challenged the swans `[CE 8.28]`; Karṇa answers in rage, then in a low voice tells him the two curses `[CE 8.29]`:
**KARṆA** — मैंने ब्राह्मण बनकर परशुराम से अस्त्र सीखे। एक दिन गुरु मेरी जंघा पर सिर रखकर सोए — एक कीड़ा मेरी जंघा छेदता रहा; मैं हिला नहीं, कि गुरु की नींद न टूटे। रक्त से वे जागे: *"इतनी पीड़ा ब्राह्मण नहीं सह सकता।"* — **"जब सबसे ज़्यादा ज़रूरत होगी, यह अस्त्र-विद्या भूल जाओगे।"** …और एक ब्राह्मण की गाय अनजाने में मारी — **"तुम्हारे रथ का पहिया धरती निगल लेगी।"**
**SCENE 9.3 — THE QUARREL** `[CE 8.46–8.49]` *(the scene no serial shows)*
Yudhiṣṭhira, wounded and shamed by Karṇa, sees Arjuna return **without having killed Karṇa**:
**YUDHIṢṬHIRA** — तेरह वर्ष तुम्हारे भरोसे जिया! …**यह गांडीव किसी और को दे दो** — जो कर्ण को मार सके!
Arjuna **draws his sword** on his brother `[CE 8.49]` — *a secret vow: whoever tells him to give up Gāṇḍīva, he must kill.* **Kṛṣṇa** catches his wrist.
**KṚṢṆA** `[CE 8.49, condensed]` — धर्म सूक्ष्म है, पार्थ… शिकारी बलाक ने एक हिंसक पशु को मारकर स्वर्ग पाया; सत्यवादी कौशिक ने सत्य बोलकर निर्दोषों को डाकुओं के हाथ मरवाया और नरक गया। …**बड़े को "तुम" (त्वम्) कह कर अपमानित करना — बिना मारे उसे मारने जैसा है।** वही करो।
Arjuna insults Yudhiṣṭhira as "तुम" — then turns the sword on **himself**. **KṚṢṆA** — अपनी प्रशंसा करो — सम्माननीय के लिए आत्म-प्रशंसा आत्महत्या के समान है। Arjuna does, weeping, and falls at Yudhiṣṭhira's feet. The brothers embrace. *Now* he will kill Karṇa.

### SEQ 10 — दुःशासन (2:03 – 2:07) `[CE 8.61]` — **HORROR ELEVATION**
Bhīma smashes Duḥśāsana's chariot, drags him down, **plants a foot on his throat, draws a sword, cuts open his chest** `[CE 8.61.6]` — and **drinks the hot blood**, tasting it again and again.
**BHĪMA** *(blood-drenched, to the world)* `[CE 8.61.7]` — **माँ का दूध, मधु और घी, उत्तम मदिरा, दिव्य जल, दूध-दही का मक्खन — सबसे बढ़कर स्वादिष्ट है यह — शत्रु का रक्त!**
Men drop their weapons and fall down in fear; some scream with their eyes shut `[CE 8.61.8–9]`: **"यह मनुष्य नहीं है!"**
**BHĪMA** *(turning to Duryodhana's banner, roaring)* `[CE 8.61.11]` — **तेरे कंठ से रक्त पी रहा हूँ, नराधम!** — *(Sabhā-oath fulfilled — Part I Scene 17.6.)*
`[NOT CE:] Draupadī arriving to wash her hair in the blood (from the play Veṇīsaṁhāra). She is NOT on the field.`

### SEQ 11 — कर्ण और अर्जुन (2:07 – 2:20) `[CE 8.63–8.67]` — **★ ACTION PEAK OF THE TRILOGY**
**SCENE 11.1 — THE FIELD CLEARS**: both armies step back; gods and demons gather unseen in the sky (rendered as cloud-banks splitting the light — one side gold, one side storm). Karṇa: *"आज या तो मैं, या अर्जुन।"*
**SCENE 11.2 — THE DUEL (8 min, three movements):**
1. **Equal** — arrow-storms meeting mid-air, sparks off bronze; Bhīma and Kṛṣṇa watch; Śalya, for once silent.
2. **The serpent** `[CE 8.66]`: Karṇa sets a fanged arrow on the string — inside it, **Aśvasena**, the snake that escaped Khāṇḍava *(Part I callback)*. It flies for Arjuna's head — **Kṛṣṇa presses the chariot down into the earth with his feet** — the horses kneel — the arrow takes **only Arjuna's diadem**; Arjuna kills the serpent.
3. **The wheel** `[CE 8.66–8.67]`: the **earth swallows Karṇa's chariot-wheel**; his weapon-knowledge fails him. He jumps down, grips the wheel.
**KARṆA** — रुको, पार्थ! धर्म कहता है — जिसका पहिया धँसा हो, उस पर प्रहार मत करो!
**KṚṢṆA** *(from the chariot — every line a hammer)* `[CE 8.67.1–5]`
> **भाग्य से, राधेय, आज तुम्हें धर्म याद आया!** नीच लोग संकट में पड़कर दैव को कोसते हैं — अपने कुकर्मों को नहीं।
> **जब तुमने, सुयोधन, दुःशासन और शकुनि ने एकवस्त्रा द्रौपदी को सभा में बुलवाया — तब तुम्हारा धर्म कहाँ गया था, कर्ण?**
> **जब पासों के ज्ञाता शकुनि ने पासे न जानने वाले युधिष्ठिर को जीता — तब धर्म कहाँ गया था?**
> **जब दुःशासन के वश में पड़ी रजस्वला कृष्णा पर तुम सभा में हँसे — तब धर्म कहाँ गया था?**
> **जब राज्य के लोभ में, गांधारराज के सहारे, तुमने पांडव को फिर से बुलाया — तब धर्म कहाँ गया था?**
**ARJUNA** — remembering each — and **fire seems to blaze from every pore of his body** `[CE 8.67.6–7]` *(rhyme with Bhīma in the Sabhā, 2.63.15)*.
**KARṆA DOES NOT BEG** `[CE 8.67.8–11]`: still wrestling the wheel, he fires the **brahmāstra**; meets Arjuna's fire-weapon with the **Vāruṇa** — clouds and darkness; Arjuna's **Vāyavya** blows them away. Karṇa's elephant-rope banner falls.
**ARJUNA** draws the **añjalika** arrow; mantra; release `[CE 8.67]`.
**120 fps, sunset** — the arrow takes **Karṇa's head**; his body falls beside the wheel; **a radiance rises from it into the setting sun** `[CE 8.67]`.
**MUSIC:** `MUS_KARNA` — the bansuri alone, then silence.
**INTERCUT — KUNTĪ** in the city, hearing the conches; she knows which son is dead. She cannot cry out. *(Echo: Part I, the arena.)*
`[NOT CE:] Bhūdevī's curse; Karṇa giving his gold tooth to a disguised Kṛṣṇa.`

### SEQ 12 — अठारहवाँ दिन (2:20 – 2:33) `[CE 9.1–9.64]`
**SCENE 12.1 — MORNING**: **Śalya** commander; **Yudhiṣṭhira — the gentle one — kills him with a spear** `[CE 9.16]`; **Sahadeva** hunts down **Śakuni** and takes his head — *"ये पासे नहीं, बाण थे"* *(Part I vow)* `[CE 9.27]`.
**SCENE 12.2 — THE LAKE** `[CE 9.28–9.32]`: Duryodhana alone, mace on shoulder, wounded, walks into **Dvaipāyana lake** and hides under the water by illusion. The Pāṇḍavas taunt him out; **Yudhiṣṭhira**: *"किसी एक से लड़ो, कोई भी शस्त्र — जीते तो राज्य तुम्हारा।"* (Kṛṣṇa's face: *you fool*.) **DURYODHANA**: *"भीम। गदा।"* **Balarāma** arrives from pilgrimage to watch his two pupils.
**SCENE 12.3 — THE MACE DUEL (6 min)** `[CE 9.54–9.58]`: two bulls; footwork patterns (gomūtraka, maṇḍala) — Duryodhana is more skilled; Bhīma is bleeding.
**KṚṢṆA** *(low, to Arjuna)* `[CE 9.57.3–17, condensed]` — भीम अधिक बलवान है, पर दुर्योधन अधिक कुशल — न्याय से यह नहीं हारेगा। **भीम ने सभा में जंघा तोड़ने की प्रतिज्ञा की थी — उसे याद दिलाओ।**
**ARJUNA** — while Bhīma watches — **slaps his own thigh** `[CE 9.57.18]`.
Bhīma sees. Duryodhana leaps — **Bhīma's mace smashes both his thighs** `[CE 9.57]`; he kicks the fallen king's head *(Part I vow)*.
**BALARĀMA** — **plough raised, coming at Bhīma** — *"नाभि के नीचे प्रहार! अधर्म!"* — Kṛṣṇa holds him `[CE 9.59]`; he leaves in disgust.
**SCENE 12.4 — DURYODHANA'S LAST WORDS** `[CE 9.60.45–52]`
**KṚṢṆA** — जयद्रथ ने वन में कृष्णा को सताया… और **बालक अभिमन्यु अकेला अनेकों के हाथों तुम्हारे दोषों से मारा गया — इसीलिए तुम रण में मारे गए।** `[CE 9.60.45–46]`
**DURYODHANA** *(on his back, broken, laughing)* `[CE 9.60.47–50]`
> मैंने विधि से अध्ययन किया, दान दिया, समुद्र तक धरती पर राज किया, शत्रुओं के सिर पर खड़ा रहा — **मुझसे अच्छा अंत किसका हुआ?** क्षत्रिय जो मृत्यु चाहते हैं — वह मुझे मिली। देवताओं के योग्य भोग भोगे… **मैं अपने मित्रों के साथ स्वर्ग जाऊँगा, अच्युत — और तुम लोग अपनी टूटी इच्छाओं के साथ शोक करते जियोगे।**
**SFX/VISUAL:** from a clear evening sky **real flowers fall on him**; music from nowhere; *"साधु! साधु!"* from the height channels `[CE 9.60.51–52]`. The Pāṇḍavas lower their eyes in shame.
**SCENE 12.5 — THE CHARIOT BURNS** `[CE 9.61.8–14]` — **RAJAMOULI ELEVATION**
At the empty Kaurava camp: **KṚṢṆA** — *"गांडीव ले कर तुम पहले उतरो, अर्जुन।"* Arjuna steps down. Then Kṛṣṇa. The moment Kṛṣṇa's foot leaves it — **the ape vanishes from the banner** *(Part II: Hanumān's promise ends)* — and **the whole chariot bursts into flame and collapses into ash**.
**KṚṢṆA** — द्रोण और कर्ण के अस्त्रों से यह कब का जल चुका था, पार्थ। मैं बैठा था — इसलिए खड़ा था।
**SCENE 12.6 — DUSK**: Duryodhana, alone among jackals, **anoints Aśvatthāman commander** with water from a pot `[CE 9.64]`.

### SEQ 13 — रात (2:33 – 2:40) `[CE 10.1–10.16]` — **HORROR PEAK**
**SCENE 13.1 — A BANYAN, NIGHT** `[CE 10.1]`: Aśvatthāman, Kṛpa, Kṛtavarman can't sleep. Above them, thousands of crows asleep in the tree — and **an owl** comes silently, killing them one by one. Aśvatthāman watches. *"यही।"*
**SCENE 13.2 — THE GATE** `[CE 10.6–10.7]`: a **colossal being** at the Pāṇḍava camp gate swallows every weapon he throws; Aśvatthāman builds a fire and **offers himself** into it — **Śiva** takes the oblation, gives him **a sword**, and **enters his body**.
**SCENE 13.3 — THE MASSACRE** `[CE 10.8]` *(no music; only breath, cloth, bodies)*
Into **Dhṛṣṭadyumna's** tent: he kicks him awake, pins him; **DHṚṢṬADYUMNA** *(choking)* `[CE 10.8.19]` — **शस्त्र से मारो, आचार्यपुत्र — कि मैं पुण्यलोक जाऊँ!** — he **stamps and beats him to death like an animal** `[CE 10.8.18]`. Then **Śikhaṇḍin**; **the five sons of Draupadī** — Prativindhya, Sutasoma, Śrutakarman, Śatānīka, Śrutasena — boys we saw born; **Uttamaujas, Yudhāmanyu**… Kṛpa and Kṛtavarman **set fire to the camp and cut down whoever runs out**.
**SCENE 13.4 — DAWN — DURYODHANA** `[CE 10.9]`: they tell the dying king. *"आज मैं इंद्र के समान हूँ"* — and dies.
**SCENE 13.5 — DRAUPADĪ** `[CE 10.11]`: on the ashes of the camp, Draupadī sits down to fast: *"अश्वत्थामा के सिर की मणि लाओ — वरना यहीं प्राण दूँगी।"* Bhīma goes; Kṛṣṇa and Arjuna follow.
**SCENE 13.6 — THE GAṄGĀ — BRAHMAŚIRAS** `[CE 10.13–10.15]`: Aśvatthāman beside Vyāsa; he takes **a reed (iṣīkā) in his left hand** and charges it as **brahmaśiras** — fire springs from it `[CE 10.13.17–20]` — *"पांडवों के विनाश के लिए"*; Arjuna counters; two suns rising over the river; **Nārada and Vyāsa** step between them. **Arjuna withdraws his** — *a brahmacārin's discipline*. Aśvatthāman **cannot** — and turns it into **the wombs of the Pāṇḍava women**.
**SCENE 13.7 — THE CURSE** `[CE 10.16]`
**KṚṢṆA** `[CE 10.16.10, 14]` — **उत्तरा का गर्भस्थ बालक जीएगा — मैं उसे जीवित करूँगा; साठ वर्ष राज करेगा।** और तुम — **तीन हज़ार वर्ष तक इस धरती पर भटकोगे — अकेले, बिना किसी साथी के, रक्त और पीप की दुर्गंध लिए।**
Aśvatthāman cuts the **jewel** from his forehead and hands it over; **Draupadī gives it to Yudhiṣṭhira to wear** `[CE 10.16]`.

# CODA — "शान्त" (2:40:00 – 3:10:00)

### SEQ 14 — स्त्रियाँ (2:40 – 2:48) `[CE 11.1–11.27]`
**SCENE 14.1 — THE IRON BHĪMA** `[CE 11.12]`: blind Dhṛtarāṣṭra opens his arms: *"भीम कहाँ है?"* — Kṛṣṇa pushes forward **an iron statue**; the old king **crushes it to powder**, then breaks down weeping: *"हाय भीम…"* — Kṛṣṇa: *"वह जीवित है, राजन्।"*
**SCENE 14.2 — GĀNDHĀRĪ** `[CE 11.15.7]`: Yudhiṣṭhira bows before her; through a gap under her blindfold her glance falls on **his toe-nails — and they turn black**.
**SCENE 14.3 — THE FIELD OF WOMEN** `[CE 11.16–11.25]` — *the most important wide shot of the trilogy*
**Vyāsa gives Gāndhārī sight** of the battlefield. **An endless plain of the dead** — widows searching among the heads; jackals; vultures; mothers holding sons. *(Callback: Draupadī's prophecy, Part I Scene 18.4 — "hair loose, smeared with their kin's blood".)*
**GĀNDHĀRĪ** *(to Kṛṣṇa)* `[CE 11.25.41]` — तुम चाहते तो रोक सकते थे… **छत्तीसवें वर्ष, मधुसूदन, तुम्हारे बंधु आपस में लड़कर मरेंगे — और तुम अकेले वन में मरोगे।**
**KṚṢṆA** *(a small, sad smile)* — जानता था। वृष्णियों को कोई और नहीं मार सकता — वे स्वयं ही नष्ट होंगे।
**SCENE 14.4 — THE COUNT** `[CE 11.26.9–10]`: **YUDHIṢṬHIRA** — **एक अरब छियासठ करोड़ बीस हज़ार — इस युद्ध में मारे गए। चौबीस हज़ार एक सौ पैंसठ का पता नहीं।**
**SCENE 14.5 — THE WATER** `[CE 11.27]`: on the Gaṅgā, offering water to the dead — **KUNTĪ** *(at last)* — **कर्ण को भी जल दो… वह तुम्हारा बड़ा भाई था।**
Yudhiṣṭhira's face. **YUDHIṢṬHIRA** `[CE 12.6.10]` — *(later, broken)* — आज से **कोई स्त्री कोई रहस्य नहीं छिपा पाएगी।**

### SEQ 15 — शरशय्या का उपदेश (2:48 – 2:54) `[CE 12–13]`
Coronation (the rākṣasa **Cārvāka** in brahmin disguise screams *"बंधु-हत्यारे!"* — the brahmins' angry "hum" burns him to ash) `[CE 12.39]`. Kṛṣṇa brings Yudhiṣṭhira to **Bhīṣma** on the arrow-bed.
**MONTAGE OF TEACHINGS** (90 sec; a handful of lines; seasons changing over the field): Rājadharma — *"राजा प्रजा के लिए है"*; Āpaddharma; Mokṣadharma; Viṣṇu's thousand names murmured at dusk `[CE 13.135]`.
**SCENE 15.1 — THE 58TH NIGHT** `[CE 13.153.27–28; 13.154]`
**BHĪṢMA** — **अट्ठावन रातें इन तीखे बाणों पर लेटे बीत गईं — जैसे सौ वर्ष।** …**यह पुण्य माघ मास आ गया है, युधिष्ठिर — यह शुक्ल पक्ष है।**
He blesses them all, closes his eyes, holds his breath in yoga; **each part of his body the life leaves becomes free of arrows — in a moment he lies arrowless** `[CE 13.154.3–4]`; a light leaves through the crown of his head; flowers fall. **Gaṅgā rises from the river weeping for her son**; Kṛṣṇa comforts her `[CE 13.154]`. *(Bookend: Part I opened with her drowning seven sons and taking the eighth.)*

### SEQ 16 — अश्वमेध और नेवला (2:54 – 2:57) `[CE 14]`
Montage: Marutta's buried gold; **Uttarā gives birth to a still child — Kṛṣṇa takes him in his arms — he breathes: Parīkṣit** `[CE 14.65–14.69]`; Arjuna following the horse; at **Maṇalūra** his son **Babhruvāhana** kills him — Ulūpī's jewel revives him `[CE 14.78–14.82]`.
**SCENE 16.1 — THE MONGOOSE** `[CE 14.92–14.96]`: after the greatest sacrifice on earth, **a mongoose, half its body golden**, rolls in the ground and speaks: *"यह यज्ञ एक भूखे ब्राह्मण परिवार के मुट्ठी भर सत्तू के बराबर भी नहीं — जो उन्होंने भूख से मरते हुए एक अतिथि को दे दिया।"* The emperor's face.

### SEQ 17 — गंगा की वह रात (2:57 – 3:01) `[CE 15]` — **EMOTIONAL ELEVATION**
**Fifteen years** later `[CE 15.4.12]`, the elders leave for the forest — Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Gāndhārī, **Kuntī**, Vidura, Sañjaya. **Vidura**, skeleton-thin, leaning on a tree, looks into Yudhiṣṭhira's eyes — and **enters him** `[CE 15.33]`.
**SCENE 17.1 — THE NIGHT OF THE DEAD** `[CE 15.39–15.41]`
Vyāsa at the Gaṅgā: *"आज रात।"* The river roars — and **out of the water walk the dead**: Bhīṣma and Droṇa first; **the five sons of Draupadī, Abhimanyu, Ghaṭotkaca; Karṇa and Duryodhana; Śakuni** `[CE 15.40.7–9]` — shining, **without anger, without enmity**.
**THE PĀṆḌAVAS MEET KARṆA** `[CE 15.41.4–5]` — as a brother. Kuntī touches his face. Draupadī holds her sons. Uttarā sees Abhimanyu.
**MUSIC:** `MUS_MAIN` played once, softly, on a single bansuri.
Dawn: they walk back into the river. *(Widows who wish follow their husbands in.)*
**SCENE 17.2 — THE FOREST FIRE** `[CE 15.45]`: Dhṛtarāṣṭra, Gāndhārī and Kuntī sit in meditation as a wildfire comes; they don't move.

### SEQ 18 — मौसल (3:01 – 3:04) `[CE 16]`
**Thirty-six years.** Boys dress **Sāmba** as a pregnant woman to mock three sages: *"यह क्या जनेगी?"* — **"यदुवंश का नाश करने वाला लोहे का मूसल"** — next morning Sāmba "gives birth" to it `[CE 16.2]`. Ground to powder, thrown in the sea; it grows on the shore as **eraka** reeds.
**PRABHĀSA**: drunk Yādavas; **Sātyaki** mocks **Kṛtavarman** for the night massacre; Kṛtavarman mocks Sātyaki for beheading armless Bhūriśravas *(Part III callbacks)*; Sātyaki beheads Kṛtavarman; the reeds become iron clubs in their hands; **everyone kills everyone** `[CE 16.4]`.
**BALARĀMA** under a tree — **a great white serpent slides out of his mouth into the sea**.
**SCENE 18.1 — KṚṢṆA** `[CE 16.5]` — *the quietest scene of the trilogy*
A forest. Kṛṣṇa lying on the ground in yoga, one foot raised. A hunter, **JARĀ**, sees something move — a deer? — **the arrow pierces the sole of his foot** `[CE 16.5.20]`. Jarā runs up, sees who it is, falls at his feet, weeping `[CE 16.5.21]`. Kṛṣṇa — no reproach — comforts him. Light fills the trees. He is gone.
**SCENE 18.2 — DVĀRAKĀ** `[CE 16.8–16.9]`: Arjuna arrives, leads the women and old men out — **the sea swallows Dvārakā behind them**. On the road, **cowherd robbers** attack: Arjuna **can barely string Gāṇḍīva**; the divine weapons don't come when called; the inexhaustible quivers **run empty** *(Part II rhyme: the Kirāta)*. **VYĀSA**: *"काल ने तुम्हें सब दिया था — काल ने ही वापस ले लिया।"*

### SEQ 19 — महाप्रस्थान (3:04 – 3:07) `[CE 17]`
**Parīkṣit** crowned in Hastinapura, **Vajra** in Indraprastha `[CE 17.1.8–9]`. Six in bark clothes — and a **dog** — walk away.
At the sea, **Agni** stands in the path: *"गांडीव लौटा दो — वरुण का है।"* Arjuna throws it into the waves `[CE 17.1]`.
The Himālaya. One by one they fall `[CE 17.2]`:
**DRAUPADĪ** — *"उसका अर्जुन के प्रति पक्षपात"* · **SAHADEVA** — *"अपनी बुद्धि का अभिमान"* · **NAKULA** — *"रूप का अभिमान"* · **ARJUNA** — *"एक दिन में सब शत्रु मारने का दंभ; दूसरे धनुर्धरों का तिरस्कार"* · **BHĪMA** — *"अति-भोजन और बल का घमंड"*. Yudhiṣṭhira never looks back.
**INDRA**'s chariot: *"आओ — पर कुत्ता नहीं।"* **YUDHIṢṬHIRA**: *"जो मेरी शरण में है, उसे नहीं छोड़ूँगा।"* The dog becomes **Dharma** `[CE 17.3]`.

### SEQ 20 — स्वर्गारोहण (3:07 – 3:09) `[CE 18.1–18.5]`
Heaven: **Duryodhana on a throne, shining** `[CE 18.1]`. *"मेरे भाई कहाँ हैं? वहीं जाऊँगा।"*
A messenger leads him down a road of corpses, razor-leaved trees, boiling rivers `[CE 18.2]`. He turns to go back — and voices cry from the dark: **"रुको… तुम्हारे होने से हमारी पीड़ा कम होती है…"** — **"मैं कर्ण हूँ… मैं भीम… अर्जुन… नकुल, सहदेव… द्रौपदी…"**
**YUDHIṢṬHIRA** — **मैं यहीं रहूँगा।**
Light. The hell is gone — **Indra and Dharma**: *"यह द्रोण से कहे छल का दंड था, पुत्र।"* `[CE 18.3]` He bathes in the heavenly river, his human body falls away; he sees them all in their true forms — **Kṛṣṇa as Viṣṇu; Karṇa beside the Sun; Bhīṣma among the Vasus** `[CE 18.4–18.5]`.

### SEQ 21 — FRAME CLOSES — "ऊर्ध्वबाहु" (3:09 – 3:10) `[CE 18.5.26–29, 49]`
**INT. SARPASATTRA, TAKṢAŚILĀ — DAWN**
Vaiśaṁpāyana falls silent. **Janamejaya** is weeping. *(The priests complete the rite; the fire is allowed to die.)* **Āstīka**, the boy, smiles: **the serpents are free** — in the grey dawn they slide away from the pit into the grass `[CE 18.5.27]`.
Janamejaya dismisses the brahmins — and rides home **from Takṣaśilā to Hastinapura** `[CE 18.5.29]`.
**VYĀSA**, alone on a ridge above the empty pavilion, raises both arms to the sky:
**VYĀSA** `[CE 18.5.49]` — **ऊर्ध्वबाहुर्विरौम्येष न च कश्चिच्छृणोति मे। धर्मादर्थश्च कामश्च स किमर्थं न सेव्यते॥**
> **मैं भुजाएँ उठाकर पुकार रहा हूँ — पर कोई नहीं सुनता: धर्म से ही अर्थ और काम मिलते हैं — फिर धर्म का पालन क्यों नहीं होता?**
**FINAL IMAGE:** the white conch from Part I's first frame, lying on the ashes, silent. The wind passes through it — a faint note.
**CHORUS (very soft):** *म… हा… भा… र… त…*
**FADE OUT.**
