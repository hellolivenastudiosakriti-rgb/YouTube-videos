The king's face. A sound comes out of him that is not a word.

# ACT I — "गीता और पितामह" (5:00 – 58:00)

### SEQ 1 — गीता (5:00 – 17:00) `[CE 6.23–6.40 = Bhagavad Gītā 1–18]`
**Staging rule:** real time stands still only in the *edit* — the armies breathe, banners flap, horses stamp; no frozen-time VFX (`[NOT CE]`). Twelve minutes, built as a dialogue between two men in a chariot.
**ARJUNA** `[CE 6.23.28–46, condensed]` — इन्हें मारकर राज्य? गुरुओं को मारने से भीख माँग कर जीना अच्छा… कुल का नाश, कुलधर्म का नाश… मैं नहीं लड़ूँगा, गोविंद।
**KṚṢṆA** *(first words — sharp, almost a slap)* `[CE 6.24.3]` — **क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ** — यह नपुंसकता तुम्हें शोभा नहीं देती। हृदय की यह तुच्छ दुर्बलता छोड़ो — उठो, परंतप!
**KṚṢṆA** `[CE 6.24.12]` — ऐसा कभी नहीं था कि मैं न था, या तुम न थे, या ये राजा न थे… और न आगे ऐसा होगा कि हम न रहें।
**KṚṢṆA** `[CE 6.24.47]` — **कर्मण्येवाधिकारस्ते मा फलेषु कदाचन** — तुम्हारा अधिकार कर्म पर है, फल पर कभी नहीं।
**KṚṢṆA** `[CE 6.26.7–8]` — जब-जब धर्म की ग्लानि होती है, भारत… साधुओं की रक्षा, दुष्टों के विनाश, धर्म की स्थापना के लिए — मैं युग-युग में प्रकट होता हूँ।
**THE FORM** `[CE 6.33 = Gītā 11]` — **RAJAMOULI ELEVATION #1 (the trilogy's largest image)**
Arjuna asks to see. Kṛṣṇa gives him a "divine eye". *(Only Arjuna — and through Vyāsa's gift, Sañjaya — sees; the armies see nothing.)*
**DESIGN (grounded, terrifying):** not a blue god with many arms — **Time**: the sky over Kurukṣetra fills with a vast shape made of fire, faces, teeth and stars; the warriors of both armies — Bhīṣma, Droṇa, Karṇa, the Kauravas, even Pāṇḍava allies — **rushing into its mouths like moths into flame**; some caught between its teeth, heads crushed `[CE 6.33.26–29]`.
**KṚṢṆA (voice from everywhere)** `[CE 6.33.32–33]` — **कालोऽस्मि लोकक्षयकृत् प्रवृद्धः** — मैं काल हूँ, लोकों का नाश करने वाला… तुम न भी लड़ो, तब भी ये योद्धा नहीं बचेंगे। **इन्हें मैंने पहले ही मार दिया है — तुम केवल निमित्त बनो, सव्यसाची।**
Arjuna, trembling, begs him to return to his human form; Kṛṣṇa does — the charioteer again, dust on his cheek.
**KṚṢṆA** `[CE 6.40.66]` — सब धर्मों को छोड़ कर मेरी शरण में आओ — मैं तुम्हें सब पापों से मुक्त करूँगा। शोक मत करो।
**ARJUNA** `[CE 6.40.73]` — **नष्टो मोहः स्मृतिर्लब्धा** — मोह नष्ट हुआ, स्मृति लौट आई, अच्युत। मैं स्थिर हूँ… आपकी आज्ञा मानूँगा।
**120 fps:** he picks up **Gāṇḍīva**. Strings it. The twang rolls across both armies. **MUSIC:** `MUS_MAIN`.

### SEQ 2 — युधिष्ठिर की पदयात्रा (17:00 – 22:00) `[CE 6.41]`
As the armies tense, **Yudhiṣṭhira takes off his armour, puts down his weapons, steps down — and walks barefoot toward the enemy.** His brothers run after him; the Kaurava ranks jeer: *"डर गया!"*
He kneels before **Bhīṣma**, then **Droṇa**, **Kṛpa**, **Śalya** — asks permission to fight, and their blessing.
**BHĪṢMA** `[CE 6.41, condensed]` — **मनुष्य धन का दास है, धन किसी का दास नहीं।** कौरवों ने मुझे धन से बाँध रखा है… इसलिए उनकी ओर से लड़ूँगा — **पर विजय तुम्हारी हो।**
(Droṇa, Kṛpa, Śalya — the same, in their own words.) On the way back Yudhiṣṭhira calls out: *"जो हमारे साथ आना चाहे — आए।"* **YUYUTSU** crosses the field to the Pāṇḍavas — the Kaurava ranks hiss `[CE 6.41]`.

### SEQ 3 — पहले नौ दिन (22:00 – 42:00) — *war montage built around five set-pieces*
**DAY 1** `[CE 6.42–6.45]` — the first collision (camera rule: never more than 3 seconds without a clear geography); **Prince Uttara**, Virāṭa's son, killed by Śalya; his brother **Śveta** cuts through the Kauravas until Bhīṣma kills him. Pāṇḍava despair at sunset.
**DAY 2** `[CE 6.46–6.51]` — **Bhīma vs the Kaliṅgas**: on foot against elephant-lines, a mace and a sword, an elephant's tusk torn out — the most physical sequence in the film.
**DAY 3 — "चक्र"** `[CE 6.52–6.55]` — **RAJAMOULI ELEVATION #2**
Bhīṣma is a storm; Arjuna fights softly — *he cannot kill his grandfather*. **Kṛṣṇa** turns to Arjuna; then, without a word, **lets go of the reins, leaps from the chariot, and runs at Bhīṣma** with the **razor-edged discus raised** `[CE 6.55.86]` — the Sudarśana "shining like a lotus on the stalk of his arm" `[CE 6.55.89]`. Kṛṣṇa roars `[CE 6.55.91]`.
**BHĪṢMA** *(lowering his bow, opening his arms)* `[CE 6.55.94]` — **आइए, आइए, देवेश, जगन्निवास — शार्ङ्ग और चक्र धारण करने वाले, आपको नमस्कार। मुझे रथ से गिरा दीजिए — आपके हाथों मरना मेरा सम्मान है।**
**Arjuna** jumps down, runs, **throws himself at Kṛṣṇa's legs** and holds him — dragged **ten steps** — at the tenth he stops him `[CE 6.55.98]`. *"प्रतिज्ञा करता हूँ — पूरी शक्ति से लड़ूँगा।"* Kṛṣṇa climbs back onto the chariot, discus in hand `[CE 6.55.101]`.
`[NOTE:] The CE uses the word "rathāṅga" (6.55.83, 94) for the discus — later tradition read it as "chariot wheel". Show the discus.`
**DAYS 4–8 (fast, emotional beats)** `[CE 6.56–6.92]`: Bhīma kills sons of Dhṛtarāṣṭra one after another (Duryodhana's face each time); **Bhūriśravas kills Sātyaki's ten sons** `[CE 6.70]`; **Irāvat**, Arjuna's son by Ulūpī, young and laughing, cut down by the rākṣasa **Alambuṣa** `[CE 6.86]` — Arjuna learns of his son's death in the middle of a duel; **Ghaṭotkaca**'s night-terror.
**DAY 9 — "चाबुक"** `[CE 6.95–6.102]`: Bhīṣma's slaughter; the Pāṇḍava army breaking; **Kṛṣṇa again jumps down — this time with only his whip** `[CE 6.102.54]`, roaring like a lion. Bhīṣma's smile. Arjuna stops him again.
**NIGHT 9 — "पितामह, आपको कैसे मारें?"** `[CE 6.103]` — *the most devastating quiet scene of the war*
The Pāṇḍavas and Kṛṣṇa walk **unarmed** through the night to the Kaurava camp, into Bhīṣma's tent. He receives them with love.
**YUDHIṢṬHIRA** — पितामह… आपके रहते हम जीत नहीं सकते। आप ही बताइए — **आपकी मृत्यु का उपाय।**
**BHĪṢMA** `[CE 6.103, condensed]` — शस्त्र हाथ में हो तो देवता भी मुझे नहीं मार सकते। पर मैं उस पर बाण नहीं चलाता जो स्त्री जन्मा था, जो शस्त्र डाल दे, जो गिर गया हो… **शिखंडी को आगे करो — उसके पीछे से अर्जुन।** …जाओ। सो जाओ। कल मुझे मारना है।
Arjuna, outside, breaks down: *"जिनकी गोद में खेला…"*

### SEQ 4 — दसवाँ दिन (42:00 – 58:00) — **RAJAMOULI ELEVATION #3 / ACT I CLIMAX**
**SCENE 4.1 — BATTLEFIELD — DAY** `[CE 6.104–6.114]`
**Śikhaṇḍin** in front of Arjuna's chariot; **Arjuna and Bhīma guard the wheels**. Bhīṣma sees Śikhaṇḍin — and **lowers his bow**. Śikhaṇḍin's arrows sting him; behind them, Arjuna's.
**BHĪṢMA** *(to Duḥśāsana, as the arrows go in — a refrain that becomes a chant)* `[CE 6.114.55–57]`
> ये बाण, जो एक के बाद एक, बिना टूटे आ रहे हैं — **ये शिखंडी के बाण नहीं।**
> जो मुझे मूसलों की तरह मार रहे हैं — **ये शिखंडी के बाण नहीं।**
> जो मेरे प्राण उखाड़ रहे हैं — **ये शिखंडी के बाण नहीं… ये अर्जुन के हैं।**
**120 fps, sunset:** Bhīṣma **falls from his chariot** — and **doesn't touch the ground**: so many arrows that he lies **on them**, held up off the earth `[CE 6.114]`.
**SFX:** both armies' noise **stops**; a single conch from nowhere; wind. Gaṅgā's sound under it (river-rush designed into the wind).
Bhīṣma, in a whisper, looks at the sun: *"दक्षिणायन है… मैं उत्तरायण की प्रतीक्षा करूँगा।"* `[CE 6.114]` *(Callback: Śaṁtanu's boon, Part I Scene 2.5.)*
**SCENE 4.2 — THE PILLOW** `[CE 6.115]`: kings rush with soft pillows; he turns his face away. *"अर्जुन — योद्धा का तकिया।"* Arjuna, weeping, **fires three arrows into the ground** under his head `[CE 6.115]`.
**SCENE 4.3 — THE WATER** `[CE 6.116]`: *"प्यास…"* — servants bring gold jugs; he refuses. Arjuna **circles him, draws, and shoots one arrow into the earth beside his face — a jet of clear water rises to his lips** `[CE 6.116]` (practical water-rig; no glow). Bhīṣma drinks. Then, to Duryodhana: *"अब भी समय है — संधि कर लो।"* Duryodhana turns away.
**SCENE 4.4 — NIGHT — KARṆA** `[CE 6.117]`
Alone, Karṇa comes and kneels by the arrow-bed, crying.
**KARṆA** — मैं राधेय… जिसे आप सदा देखकर क्रोधित होते थे।
**BHĪṢMA** *(opening his eyes, putting a hand on his head like a father)* `[CE 6.117.9]` — **तुम कौंतेय हो, राधेय नहीं। मुझे नारद से पता था।** …मैंने तुम्हें कठोर इसलिए कहा कि तुम्हारा तेज मंद हो, कुल में फूट न पड़े। मुझे तुमसे कोई द्वेष नहीं। …यदि हो सके तो भाइयों से मिल जाओ।
**KARṆA** — नहीं, पितामह। …मुझे आज्ञा दीजिए — लड़ने की।
**BHĪṢMA** — जाओ। स्वर्ग के लिए लड़ो।
*(Karṇa has kept his Udyoga vow: he enters the war only now.)*

# ACT II — "चक्रव्यूह और रात्रि" (58:00 – 1:55:00)

### SEQ 5 — द्रोण की प्रतिज्ञा (58:00 – 66:00) `[CE 7.1–7.28]`
**Droṇa** anointed commander `[CE 7.5]`: *"युधिष्ठिर को जीवित पकड़ लाऊँगा — यदि अर्जुन दूर हो"* `[CE 7.11–7.12]`.
**DAY 12** — the **Saṁśaptakas** (Trigarta suicide-warriors) swear a death-oath at a fire and drag Arjuna south `[CE 7.16–7.17]`.
**BHAGADATTA** `CH_BHAGADATTA` — the ancient king of Prāgjyotiṣa — on the colossal elephant **Supratīka**, flattening Pāṇḍava lines. `[NOT CE:] his wrinkled eyelids tied up with a band (*224).` Arjuna returns. Bhagadatta hurls the **Vaiṣṇava weapon** — **Kṛṣṇa stands up in the chariot and takes it on his chest**; it becomes **a garland (Vaijayantī)** `[CE 7.28.18]`. *"यह अस्त्र केवल मैं ही झेल सकता था, पार्थ।"* Arjuna kills elephant and king.

### SEQ 6 — अभिमन्यु (66:00 – 82:00) `[CE 7.33–7.57]` — **TRAGIC PEAK OF ACT II**
**SCENE 6.1 — DAWN, DAY 13**: Arjuna again dragged away by the Saṁśaptakas. **Droṇa forms the cakravyūha** — a revolving wheel of chariots `[CE 7.33]`.
**YUDHIṢṬHIRA** — इसे केवल अर्जुन, कृष्ण, प्रद्युम्न… या तुम तोड़ सकते हो, अभिमन्यु।
**ABHIMANYU** `[CE 7.34.18–19]` — पिता के विजय की कामना से मैं द्रोण के इस अभेद्य व्यूह में घुसूँगा और उसे तोड़ूँगा। **पिताजी ने मुझे व्यूह भेदना सिखाया है — पर किसी संकट में बाहर निकलना मैं नहीं जानता।**
**YUDHIṢṬHIRA** `[CE 7.34.20]` — तुम द्वार बना दो — हम सब तुम्हारे पीछे-पीछे आएँगे।
`[NOT CE:] learning in Subhadrā's womb.`
**SCENE 6.2 — THE BREACH**: Abhimanyu's chariot hits the wheel — and **breaks in** (120 fps; spokes, dust, a boy's war-cry). Behind him the Pāṇḍavas charge the gap — and **JAYADRATHA** closes it: *"शिव का वरदान"* — one day, all Pāṇḍavas but Arjuna `[CE 7.41]` *(callback: Part II Scene 13.4)*.
**SCENE 6.3 — INSIDE THE WHEEL**: alone, sixteen, he is unstoppable: **Duryodhana's son Lakṣmaṇa** dies `[CE 7.45]`; Duryodhana screams.
**DROṆA** *(watching, almost proud)* — इसके हाथ में धनुष रहते कोई इसे नहीं जीत सकता… **इसका धनुष काटो, पीछे से। घोड़े मारो। सारथी मारो।** `[CE 7.47, condensed]`
**SIX WARRIORS** surround him `[CE 7.46]`. **Karṇa — from behind — cuts his bow** `[CE 7.47]`; horses fall; charioteers fall.
Abhimanyu leaps down with **sword and shield** — Droṇa shoots the sword-hilt out of his hand; Karṇa cuts the shield.
He grabs **a chariot wheel** and whirls it — Kṛṣṇa's discus in a boy's hands — the frame rhymes with Day 3 `[CE 7.47–7.48]`.
They cut the wheel apart. He takes **a mace**. **Duḥśāsana's son** comes at him with a mace; both fall; **the other rises first — and brings the mace down on Abhimanyu's head** `[CE 7.48]`.
**SFX:** nothing for four seconds. Then a single bird.
**SCENE 6.4 — EVENING — ARJUNA RETURNS** `[CE 7.50–7.51]`: the camp silent; no one meets his eyes. *"अभिमन्यु कहाँ है?"* Then he learns of Jayadratha at the gate.
**ARJUNA** `[CE 7.51, condensed]` — **कल सूर्यास्त से पहले यदि जयद्रथ को न मारूँ — तो स्वयं अग्नि में प्रवेश करूँगा।** He sounds Devadatta; Kṛṣṇa sounds Pāñcajanya; across the field Jayadratha trembles.
**SCENE 6.5 — NIGHT — THE DREAM** `[CE 7.57]`: Arjuna and Kṛṣṇa walk, in a dream, up the white mountain to **Śiva** at a lake of nectar; the Pāśupata is renewed. Arjuna wakes with dew on him.

### SEQ 7 — चौदहवाँ दिन और रात (82:00 – 1:42:00) `[CE 7.63–7.156]` — **RAJAMOULI ELEVATION #4–#5**
**SCENE 7.1 — THE CHARGE**: Arjuna cuts through Droṇa's formations (Droṇa: *"गुरु से नहीं लड़ोगे?"* — Arjuna: *"आप मेरे गुरु हैं, शत्रु नहीं"* — he goes around him). Horses exhausted — **Arjuna makes a pool of water with arrows; Kṛṣṇa unyokes the horses, pulls out arrows from their bodies, bathes them in the middle of the battlefield** while Arjuna holds off armies alone.
**SCENE 7.2 — SĀTYAKI & BHŪRIŚRAVAS** `[CE 7.117–7.118]`: Sātyaki, exhausted, dragged by the hair, sword at his throat — **Arjuna cuts off Bhūriśravas's arm** from afar. Bhūriśravas, one-armed, sits down in yoga, reproaching Arjuna — and **Sātyaki beheads him**. Everyone condemns it. *(Seed of Mausala: Kṛtavarman will throw this at Sātyaki in Prabhāsa.)*
**SCENE 7.3 — SUNSET** `[CE 7.121.12–20]`
Arjuna reaches Jayadratha — the sun "going swiftly" toward the western mountain `[CE 7.121.15]`.
**KṚṢṆA** *(urgent)* `[CE 7.121.16–20]` — **धनंजय, इस दुरात्मा सैंधव का सिर काटो — सूर्य अस्त होने वाला है!** और सुनो — इसके पिता **वृद्धक्षत्र** ने वर पाया था: जो युद्ध में इसका सिर काटकर धरती पर गिराएगा, उसके सिर के सौ टुकड़े होंगे। …सिर गिरने मत देना।
**THE SHOT (120 fps):** Arjuna's arrow takes the head — and a chain of arrows **carries it through the sky like a bird** — over the armies — over a ridge — into the lap of **an old king praying with closed eyes**, far outside the field (Vṛddhakṣatra) `[CE 7.121]`. **SCENE 7.4 — THE NIGHT BATTLE** `[CE 7.129–7.154]` *(the rules break — first time fighting after dark)*: torches on every chariot; elephants with fire-pots; **GHAṬOTKACA** grows in the dark, rains rocks and illusions; Kaurava lines dissolve in panic; **Duryodhana** begs Karṇa: *"इसे मारो — वरना कोई नहीं बचेगा!"*
**KARṆA** *(looking at the spear meant for Arjuna)* — … **Indra's Śakti** leaves his hand like a meteor — strikes Ghaṭotkaca's heart; the giant, dying, **swells to mountain-size and falls on an akṣauhiṇī** `[CE 7.154]`.
**SCENE 7.5 — KṚṢṆA DANCES** `[CE 7.155.3]`: the Pāṇḍavas weeping over Bhīma's son — and **Kṛṣṇa dancing, laughing, roaring, "like a tree shaken by wind"**. Arjuna, appalled. **KṚṢṆA** `[CE 7.155–7.156, condensed]` — **कर्ण के पास वह शक्ति थी जो तुम्हें मारती।** अब वह मनुष्य है — मारा जा सकता है। …तुम्हारे लिए ही मैंने जरासंध, शिशुपाल और निषाद एकलव्य को पहले ही हटाया था।

### SEQ 8 — द्रोण (1:42:00 – 1:55:00) `[CE 7.157–7.173]` — **MORAL CRISIS**
**SCENE 8.1 — DAY 15**: **Droṇa kills Drupada and Virāṭa** `[CE 7.161]`. Pāñcāla lines broken.
**KṚṢṆA** — जब तक इनके हाथ में शस्त्र है, इन्हें देवता भी नहीं जीत सकते। **कहो कि अश्वत्थामा मारा गया।** Arjuna refuses. **Bhīma kills an elephant named Aśvatthāman** and bellows it `[CE 7.164]`. Droṇa, shaken, turns to the one man who never lies.
**DROṆA** — युधिष्ठिर… सच बताओ।
**YUDHIṢṬHIRA** `[CE 7.164.104–107]` — prompted by Kṛṣṇa, drowning in fear of the lie and clinging to victory — **"अश्वत्थामा मारा गया"** — and, low, indistinct — **"…हाथी।"**
**SFX:** a sound like a bone breaking under the earth — his chariot, which always ran **four fingers above the ground**, **drops; the horses' hooves touch the earth** `[CE 7.164.107]`.
Droṇa lowers his bow. Puts down his weapons. **Sits in yoga** on the chariot.
**DHṚṢṬADYUMNA** climbs up with a sword. **ARJUNA** *(shouting, running)* — **जीवित लाओ! आचार्य को मत मारो!** — **Dhṛṣṭadyumna takes Droṇa's head** `[CE 7.165]`. *(Callback: Part I, the fire-voice — "born to kill Droṇa".)*
**SCENE 8.2 — NĀRĀYAṆA** `[CE 7.166–7.171]`: **Aśvatthāman**, mad with grief, releases the **Nārāyaṇa weapon** — the sky fills with fire-darts that strike whoever resists. **KṚṢṆA** — **शस्त्र डालो! रथों से उतरो!** The whole army drops weapons and steps down; the fire passes over them. **Bhīma refuses** — *"मैं भीम हूँ!"* — and is engulfed; Kṛṣṇa and Arjuna **drag him off his chariot by force**; the weapon dies.
**SCENE 8.3 — NIGHT**: the Pāṇḍava camp tears at itself — Arjuna vs Dhṛṣṭadyumna, Sātyaki vs Dhṛṣṭadyumna — over Droṇa's death. Yudhiṣṭhira alone, staring at his chariot touching the ground.
